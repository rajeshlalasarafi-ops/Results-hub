Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Results Hub India ("we", "our", "the site") handles information when you visit this website.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, phone number, email address, hall ticket number or marks. When you open a result link, you leave our site and enter your details on the official website, which has its own privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

The site may save your theme choice (dark or light) in your browser so it is remembered on your next visit. This stays on your device.

Cookies and advertising

We may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to show ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or visit aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies. You can also learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Links to other websites

Our site links to official websites run by others. We do not control their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies.

Children

This site is meant for students, parents and teachers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please use our Contact page.
