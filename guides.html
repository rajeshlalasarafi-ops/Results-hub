How to check your result online: guides and FAQs

Download PDF

Results are published by each board or exam authority on its own website. The steps differ a little by site, but most follow the same pattern. Use this guide to get ready, and use the links on our home page to reach the official sites.

Before you start

  • Keep your hall ticket or admit card ready. It has your roll number or application number.
  • Know your date of birth exactly as it was entered in your application.
  • Use a stable internet connection. Result sites are very busy on result day, so try again after a few minutes if the page is slow.
  • Make sure you are on the official website. Check the address in your browser before typing any details.

General steps to check a result

  1. Open the official result website from our home page.
  2. Find the link for your exam and year, for example "Class 10 Result" or "NEET UG Result".
  3. Enter your roll number or application number, and any other detail the site asks for, such as date of birth or a captcha.
  4. Submit the form and wait for the result page to load.
  5. Download or print the result for your records.

Other ways to get your result

Many boards also make results available through DigiLocker, and some give them through SMS or the UMANG app. Availability differs by board and year, so check the board's own notice to see which options it offers.

Online result and marksheet: what is the difference?

The result you see online is usually a provisional or online copy. The original marksheet and certificate are issued later by your school, college or board. Keep the online copy safe until you receive the original.

Understanding exam dates on this site

  • Listed by NTA: the National Testing Agency has published a date. It can still change, so check the official notice.
  • Date sheet or timetable awaited: the board has not yet published the timetable. Any month we mention is only a past-year pattern.
  • Not announced: there is no official date yet.

Frequently asked questions

My result is not opening. What should I do?

Wait a few minutes and try again, because official sites can be overloaded on result day. Try a different browser or device. If your board offers DigiLocker, try there too.

I forgot my roll number or hall ticket number.

Look at your admit card or hall ticket. If you cannot find it, check the official website of your board or exam, because many have a "know your roll number" or hall ticket download option. Otherwise, contact your school or exam centre.

My name, date of birth or marks look wrong.

Do not panic. Contact your school or the board's helpline and follow their correction process. Errors in the online copy are normally fixed through the board or exam authority.

Can I ask for rechecking or revaluation?

Most boards and some exams allow rechecking, verification or revaluation for a fee within a limited time after the result. Read the official notice for your exam to see if it is available, the last date and the fee.

Are the dates on this site official?

We copy dates from official announcements and mark anything that is only expected. Dates change, so always confirm on the official website. See our About page for more.

Do you show my result on this site?

No. We only link to the official website. We never ask for or store your hall ticket number or marks.

Tips for students and parents

  • Do not share your hall ticket number or date of birth with unknown websites or messages.
  • Be careful of messages promising "early" or "leaked" results. Only the official website is reliable.
  • Note the last dates for rechecking and for counselling or admission steps, as they can come soon after the result.
