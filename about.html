About Results Hub India

Results Hub India is a free, independent website that helps students and parents find the official result pages and exam dates for school boards, entrance exams and university results across India.

What we do

Every year, students lose time searching for the right website to check results such as CBSE, state SSC and Intermediate boards, NEET, JEE Main, CUET, EAPCET (EMCET) and POLYCET. We collect the official links in one place and explain, in plain language, how to use them.

What we do not do

  • We do not store, host or show your marks. Results are always shown on the official website.
  • We do not ask for your hall ticket number, roll number or date of birth.
  • We are not a government body and are not connected to any board, university or testing agency.

Exam dates

We list dates that exam bodies have published, and clearly mark dates that are only expected or not yet announced. Dates can change, so always confirm on the official website before you plan.

Disclaimer

We try to keep links and information correct, but boards sometimes change their web addresses and schedules without notice. Results Hub India is not responsible for decisions made using this information. If you find a wrong link or date, please tell us and we will fix it.
